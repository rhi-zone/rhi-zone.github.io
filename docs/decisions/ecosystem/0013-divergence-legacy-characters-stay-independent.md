# ADR-0013: Divergence and legacy characters stay independent

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-05-29

**Context.** Divergence is the same fork-point as the legacy world. Parallel characters (e.g. divergence Noa vs legacy Noa Chen) arise. A rule was needed for whether the two worlds' characters connect.

**Decision.** Characters in divergence are independent of legacy characters — do not cross them. Parallel characters may coincide but the parallel must never be made explicit in either world's content.

**Alternatives rejected.**
- *Make the cross-world parallel explicit (treat the parallel characters as the same person across the two worlds)* — They are meant to be kept independent; making the parallel explicit in either world's content is prohibited so each world stands on its own

**Consequences.** Content in divergence may not reference or depend on legacy characters; shared 'soul' parallels stay implicit and uncrossed. Mined from: /home/me/git/paragarden/divergence/CLAUDE.md (17).
