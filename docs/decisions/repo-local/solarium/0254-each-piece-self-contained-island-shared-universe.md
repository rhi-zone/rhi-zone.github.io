# ADR-0254: Each piece is a self-contained island; no shared universe

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-05-29

**Context.** Defining the creative practice of solarium: whether works accumulate into a connected world/canon or stand alone. A shared universe creates ongoing obligations and dependencies between pieces.

**Decision.** Every piece is self-contained and complete in itself; no piece is load-bearing for any other. No shared universe, no ongoing world, no accumulated narrative debt. The premise is the whole justification.

**Alternatives rejected.**
- *A shared universe / ongoing world where pieces connect and accumulate narrative* — It creates accumulated narrative debt and makes pieces load-bearing for each other; the practice explicitly forecloses this to keep each piece complete on its own.

**Consequences.** Future works must justify themselves on their own premise; cross-references or continuity between pieces are out of bounds. Pieces can be shipped and abandoned independently with no maintenance obligation to others.
