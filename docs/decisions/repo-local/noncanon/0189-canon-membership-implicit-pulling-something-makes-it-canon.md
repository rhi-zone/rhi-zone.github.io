# ADR-0189: Canon membership is implicit: pulling something in makes it canon

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-05-29

**Context.** noncanon answers 'what does it mean to accept a piece of world-content into your local canon?'. A central question was whether canon membership needs an explicit recorded marker per object, or whether it is implied by presence.

**Decision.** Canon metadata is implicit — if you pulled it in, it's canon. No explicit canon record is kept.

**Alternatives rejected.**
- *Maintain an explicit per-object canon record/flag* — Redundant: the act of pulling content into your local copy already constitutes the act of endorsing it as canon, so a separate record is unnecessary.

**Consequences.** There is no separate canon ledger to keep in sync; canon is derived from what is present locally. 'Canon acceptance' equals 'pulled in'.
