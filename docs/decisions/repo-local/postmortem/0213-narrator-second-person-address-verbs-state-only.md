# ADR-0213: No narrator and no second-person address; verbs of state only

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-05-29

**Context.** The project needed a voice for its entries. The conventional way to render a place-based exploratory experience is a guiding narrator or second-person immersion ('you walk into the room'), which orients the reader and supplies presence. But the premise is a world with no agents and the reader as the sole missing referent.

**Decision.** Documents describe; they do not address. No narrator, no omniscient observer, no archaeologist/alien/ghost, and no 'you.' Entries use verbs of state (things are; nothing happens). The reader's humanity is the entire mechanism and the text does not acknowledge them.

**Alternatives rejected.**
- *Second-person immersive narration ('you walk into the room') / an in-world narrator or observer* — Addressing the reader or supplying a narrator would insert an agent into a world defined by the absence of agents, and would do the reader's work of feeling themselves as the missing referent — collapsing the mechanism the project depends on.

**Consequences.** All entries are written in stative, non-addressing prose; this also forecloses the unresolved TODO option of having the page acknowledge the reader. Open worldbuilding question (POV/address) remains in TODO but is bounded by this rule.
