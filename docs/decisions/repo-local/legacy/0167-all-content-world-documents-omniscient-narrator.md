# ADR-0167: All content is in-world documents; no omniscient narrator

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-05-29

**Context.** Worldbuilding could be conveyed through an external narrator explaining the world, or through artifacts that exist inside the world. A voice contract was needed for all content.

**Decision.** Everything is a document someone in the world wrote (reports, manifestos, histories, profiles); there is no omniscient narrator. Character cards are built as in-world artifacts (observation log, interview transcript, survey, room inventory) that reveal character through evidence, explicitly preferred over a novelistic second-person portrait.

**Alternatives rejected.**
- *Omniscient narrator / novelistic second-person portrait that explains the world or character* — A portrait is 'the author explaining the character'; an in-world document is 'the character existing,' which is preferred. An omniscient narrator is disallowed outright.

**Consequences.** Every piece of content must be framed as an artifact authored from within the world; the author's explanatory voice is excluded from legacy (it belongs on ptera.world). Drives the character-card-as-artifact workflow. Mined from: /home/me/git/paragarden/legacy/CLAUDE.md (129).
