# Out-of-scope stance

**Project(s) touched:** all

**Status:** Open — stance articulated twice across two repos, never codified

**Surfaced in:** session `6842c90d` (io, 2026-04-25: "the moment we call something out of scope is the moment we fail"); also `e6ae6069` (crescent, 2026-04-26: "doing things halfway is unacceptable? similarly with delaying anything indefinitely ... with the excuse of 'it's out of scope'")

---

## The question

Should the ecosystem-wide CLAUDE.md explicitly forbid "out of scope" as a
deferral pattern? And how does such a rule interact with legitimate
project-boundary discipline (no path deps, independent repos)?

## Why this is a registry thread

A codified stance would propagate to all repos. The tension is real: the user
treats "out of scope" as a failure tell, yet the ecosystem is deliberately built
from independent, bounded projects — so a blanket ban could collide with the
boundary discipline that keeps repos decoupled.

## What's still open

- The stance is asserted but never written as a rule.
- The boundary between "evasive deferral" and "correct project boundary" is
  undefined.

None.
