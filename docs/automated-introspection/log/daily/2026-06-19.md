- User opened by establishing the specific reading they wanted: not the classical "God is absent or powerless," but the **attention-scarcity problem** — "imagine having to resolve every issue yourself. for all eight billion people."
- Explored the mechanics of omnipotence under real constraints: how precisely must a command be specified to work? Tested the cancer-eradication edge case as a stress test — "eliminating cancer is a net positive, right?" — and worked through failure modes: under-specified commands, moving genetic targets, the knock-on implications of zero cellular-error rate for evolution and speciation, and the cost of hand-authoring every species forever.

- Later threads: why would a god relinquish control ("To be surprised"); what does an atemporal god even *do*; and the intervention-as-pet-keeping framing vs. the cancer argument reconsidered under the atemporal lens.
